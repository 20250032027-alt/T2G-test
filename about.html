The Company

Get in Touch

Founded in May 2017 by five fellow developmental workers in Jasaan, Misamis Oriental, Trunk to Gold Agribusiness started from a simple question: why is Misamis Oriental sitting on one of the Philippines' most abundant coconut regions, and yet all that comes out of it is copra and tuba?

The answer became T2G. We produce coconut sugar under the CocoRush brand, along with coconut-sap seasonings (Coco Aminos, Coco Savor and Coco Balsamic), virgin coconut oil, coconut flour, Adlai, and VCO-based balms and oils. The coconut sap is gathered by our partner tappers in the barangays of Balingasag, Claveria and Jasaan, and everything is processed at our mechanized facility in San Isidro.

The name says it all. Trunk, for the coconut palm. Gold, for what we believe it is worth when treated right.

Our Dream and our Being

We are committed to supplying our customers with the finest food products produced in the Philippines, from our farms to your table.
We strive to be a globally recognized company known for its world-class food selection, quality, and safety.
Through our work, we are committed to making a real difference in the lives of our partner coconut tappers, their families, and the communities of Misamis Oriental.

Why T2G?

Our Experience

T2G was built by development workers who came out of the cooperative sector and knew the farming communities firsthand. We were among the first to commercially produce coconut sugar in Misamis Oriental, and since 2017 we have taken the process from a wok on a stove to a fully mechanized facility.

Our Quality

The sap is tested before it enters production. The facility runs regular Good Manufacturing Practices training. Our packaging is ISO-certified. We are FDA Philippines-compliant. And we test with internationally accredited labs to make sure what is on the label matches what is in the product.

Our Presence

T2G products are available through select health food stores, restaurants, and cafes, and ship to institutional buyers nationwide. We are also experienced exporters to Asia, the Middle East, Europe, the United States, and Canada, with ambitions to grow that reach further.
